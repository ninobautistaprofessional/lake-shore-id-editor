# 06 — Student Guide (Public Portal)

You do **not** need an account to use the student portal. Everything on this page
works from a normal browser with no sign-in.

## 6.1 Creating your ID for the first time

### Step 1 — Open the portal

Go to the student portal address your school gave you (for a local install:
`http://localhost:5173`).

### Step 2 — First, check whether you already have one

Before typing anything, look for the **Search** box on the form. Enter your
**Student Number** (or your ID number, or your LRN) and press **Search**.

| Result | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Found** | You already have an ID record | Do **not** submit the form again. Use **Report Lost ID** if your card is lost, or contact the registrar for a reprint |
| **Student Not Found** | No record matches that number | Continue to Step 3 and create your ID |

This search is deliberately limited: it only ever looks for the exact number you
typed, and it only returns the few fields needed to fill the form. It will never
show you anybody else's information.

### Step 3 — Choose your department

| Department | What you will be asked for |
|---|---|
| **College** | Your course (chosen from the 8 listed programmes) and academic year |
| **Junior High School** | Grade level (7–10), section and school year |
| **Senior High School** | Grade level (11–12), section and school year |

Fields that do not apply to your department are cleared automatically.

### Step 4 — Fill in your details

| Field | Who needs it |
|---|---|
| Full name | Everyone — this is what prints on the card, in uppercase |
| Course | College only |
| Grade level and section | Junior High and Senior High only |
| Student number | Everyone |
| Student ID number | Everyone |
| LRN | Basic Education only — exactly 12 digits if you enter it |
| Academic year / school year | Everyone |
| Home address (2 lines) | Everyone — prints on the back |
| Emergency contact name and phone | Everyone — prints on the back |
| Photo | Everyone — PNG, JPG or WEBP |

### Step 5 — Upload a clear photo

* Use a plain background with your face clearly visible.
* PNG, JPG or WEBP only.
* Keep the file under 10 MB and at least 96 × 96 pixels.
* If your photo has a background you want removed, the system can cut it out for
  you — ask the registrar, or use the photo processing tool in the staff app.

### Step 6 — Check the preview

The front and back of your card update live as you type. Read it carefully —
this is the last chance to fix a typo before submitting.

### Step 7 — Submit

Press **Submit ID**. You will get a **reference number**. Keep it: the registrar
may ask for it.

## 6.2 If you already have an ID

* **Lost it?** Use **Report Lost ID** below — do not create a new one.
* **Name spelled wrong, or a detail changed?** Contact the registrar; staff can
  edit your existing record, and the change is recorded in the audit log.
* **Want a reprint?** Submit a lost-ID request with a receipt. Staff approve it,
  print a new card, and mark it as a reprint in the print history.

## 6.3 Reporting a lost ID

1. Open the **Report Lost ID** form.
2. Enter your name, department and the details of the lost card.
3. Upload your **receipt** or any supporting image (PNG, JPG or PDF).
4. Submit, and keep the reference number.

> A lost-ID request can only be filed for an ID that already exists in the
> system. If yours does not exist yet, create it first.

## 6.4 If you see "Too many submissions from your network"

The portal allows a limited number of requests from one internet address to keep
it safe from spam and automated submissions.

* Wait for the countdown shown in the message and try again.
* If you are in a computer lab where many students share one address, ask the
  office to raise the limit, or solve the captcha challenge when it is offered —
  that unlocks a few more attempts for your address.

The limit is **5 requests every 10 minutes per address** by default.

## 6.5 Frequently asked questions

**Q — Do I need to create an account?**
No. The student portal needs no login.

**Q — I submitted twice. Do I have two IDs?**
No. The system rejects a second submission when the same name and identifier are
found, and the registrar can confirm your single record.

**Q — Can I see other students' information through the search?**
No. The search only answers about the exact number you typed, and only returns
the fields needed to fill your own form.

**Q — I made a mistake. Can I edit my ID myself?**
Not from the portal. Contact the registrar — they can edit it, and the change is
logged with their name and the time.

**Q — My name is printed in all caps. Why?**
That is the official ID design for the name band. It keeps the printed name
uniform and easy to read.

**Q — What if I lose internet connection mid-submission?**
Nothing is saved until you press Submit. Reopen the form and submit again; a
partially submitted form cannot create a half record.
